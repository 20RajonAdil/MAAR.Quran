Surah An-Nasr — Divine Support

سُوْرَةُ النَّصْرِ

Surah 110 of 1143 versesMedinan

Also searched as: Nasr

Listen & read in the appSearch the Qur'an
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful
  1. 1

    إِذَا جَآءَ نَصۡرُ ٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡفَتۡحُ

    When the victory of Allah has come and the conquest

  2. 2

    وَرَأَيۡتَ ٱلنَّاسَ يَدۡخُلُونَ فِي دِينِ ٱللَّهِ أَفۡوَاجࣰ ا

    And you see the people entering into the religion of Allah in multitudes

  3. 3

    فَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡهُۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ تَوَّابَۢا

    Then exalt [Him] with praise of your Lord and ask forgiveness of Him. Indeed, He is ever Accepting of repentance
