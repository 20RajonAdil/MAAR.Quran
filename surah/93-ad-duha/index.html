Surah Ad-Duha — The Morning Hours

سُوْرَةُ الضُّحٰي

Surah 93 of 11411 versesMeccan

Also searched as: Duha, Dhuha

Listen & read in the appSearch the Qur'an
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful
  1. 1

    وَٱلضُّحَىٰ

    By the morning brightness

  2. 2

    وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا سَجَىٰ

    And [by] the night when it covers with darkness

  3. 3

    مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَىٰ

    Your Lord has not taken leave of you, [O Muhammad], nor has He detested [you]

  4. 4

    وَلَلۡأٓخِرَةُ خَيۡرࣱ لَّكَ مِنَ ٱلۡأُولَىٰ

    And the Hereafter is better for you than the first [life]

  5. 5

    وَلَسَوۡفَ يُعۡطِيكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرۡضَىٰٓ

    And your Lord is going to give you, and you will be satisfied

  6. 6

    أَلَمۡ يَجِدۡكَ يَتِيمࣰ ا فَـَٔاوَىٰ

    Did He not find you an orphan and give [you] refuge

  7. 7

    وَوَجَدَكَ ضَآلࣰّ ا فَهَدَىٰ

    And He found you lost and guided [you]

  8. 8

    وَوَجَدَكَ عَآئِلࣰ ا فَأَغۡنَىٰ

    And He found you poor and made [you] self-sufficient

  9. 9

    فَأَمَّا ٱلۡيَتِيمَ فَلَا تَقۡهَرۡ

    So as for the orphan, do not oppress [him]

  10. 10

    وَأَمَّا ٱلسَّآئِلَ فَلَا تَنۡهَرۡ

    And as for the petitioner, do not repel [him]

  11. 11

    وَأَمَّا بِنِعۡمَةِ رَبِّكَ فَحَدِّثۡ

    But as for the favor of your Lord, report [it]
