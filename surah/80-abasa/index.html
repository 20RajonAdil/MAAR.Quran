Surah 'Abasa — He frowned

سُوْرَةُ عَبَسَ

Surah 80 of 11442 versesMeccan
Listen & read in the appSearch the Qur'an
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful
  1. 1

    عَبَسَ وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ

    The Prophet frowned and turned away

  2. 2

    أَن جَآءَهُ ٱلۡأَعۡمَىٰ

    Because there came to him the blind man, [interrupting]

  3. 3

    وَمَا يُدۡرِيكَ لَعَلَّهُۥ يَزَّكَّىٰٓ

    But what would make you perceive, [O Muhammad], that perhaps he might be purified

  4. 4

    أَوۡ يَذَّكَّرُ فَتَنفَعَهُ ٱلذِّكۡرَىٰٓ

    Or be reminded and the remembrance would benefit him

  5. 5

    أَمَّا مَنِ ٱسۡتَغۡنَىٰ

    As for he who thinks himself without need

  6. 6

    فَأَنتَ لَهُۥ تَصَدَّىٰ

    To him you give attention

  7. 7

    وَمَا عَلَيۡكَ أَلَّا يَزَّكَّىٰ

    And not upon you [is any blame] if he will not be purified

  8. 8

    وَأَمَّا مَن جَآءَكَ يَسۡعَىٰ

    But as for he who came to you striving [for knowledge]

  9. 9

    وَهُوَ يَخۡشَىٰ

    While he fears [Allah]

  10. 10

    فَأَنتَ عَنۡهُ تَلَهَّىٰ

    From him you are distracted

  11. 11

    كَلَّآ إِنَّهَا تَذۡكِرَةࣱ‏

    No! Indeed, these verses are a reminder

  12. 12

    فَمَن شَآءَ ذَكَرَهُۥ

    So whoever wills may remember it

  13. 13

    فِي صُحُفࣲ مُّكَرَّمَةࣲ‏

    [It is recorded] in honored sheets

  14. 14

    مَّرۡفُوعَةࣲ مُّطَهَّرَةِۭ

    Exalted and purified

  15. 15

    بِأَيۡدِي سَفَرَةࣲ‏

    [Carried] by the hands of messenger-angels

  16. 16

    كِرَامِۭ بَرَرَةࣲ‏

    Noble and dutiful

  17. 17

    قُتِلَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَآ أَكۡفَرَهُۥ

    Cursed is man; how disbelieving is he

  18. 18

    مِنۡ أَيِّ شَيۡءٍ خَلَقَهُۥ

    From what substance did He create him

  19. 19

    مِن نُّطۡفَةٍ خَلَقَهُۥ فَقَدَّرَهُۥ

    From a sperm-drop He created him and destined for him

  20. 20

    ثُمَّ ٱلسَّبِيلَ يَسَّرَهُۥ

    Then He eased the way for him

  21. 21

    ثُمَّ أَمَاتَهُۥ فَأَقۡبَرَهُۥ

    Then He causes his death and provides a grave for him

  22. 22

    ثُمَّ إِذَا شَآءَ أَنشَرَهُۥ

    Then when He wills, He will resurrect him

  23. 23

    كَلَّا لَمَّا يَقۡضِ مَآ أَمَرَهُۥ

    No! Man has not yet accomplished what He commanded him

  24. 24

    فَلۡيَنظُرِ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ إِلَىٰ طَعَامِهِۦٓ

    Then let mankind look at his food

  25. 25

    أَنَّا صَبَبۡنَا ٱلۡمَآءَ صَبࣰّ ا

    How We poured down water in torrents

  26. 26

    ثُمَّ شَقَقۡنَا ٱلۡأَرۡضَ شَقࣰّ ا

    Then We broke open the earth, splitting [it with sprouts]

  27. 27

    فَأَنۢبَتۡنَا فِيهَا حَبࣰّ ا

    And caused to grow within it grain

  28. 28

    وَعِنَبࣰ ا وَقَضۡبࣰ ا

    And grapes and herbage

  29. 29

    وَزَيۡتُونࣰ ا وَنَخۡلࣰ ا

    And olive and palm trees

  30. 30

    وَحَدَآئِقَ غُلۡبࣰ ا

    And gardens of dense shrubbery

  31. 31

    وَفَٰكِهَةࣰ وَأَبࣰّ ا

    And fruit and grass

  32. 32

    مَّتَٰعࣰ ا لَّكُمۡ وَلِأَنۡعَٰمِكُمۡ

    [As] enjoyment for you and your grazing livestock

  33. 33

    فَإِذَا جَآءَتِ ٱلصَّآخَّةُ

    But when there comes the Deafening Blast

  34. 34

    يَوۡمَ يَفِرُّ ٱلۡمَرۡءُ مِنۡ أَخِيهِ

    On the Day a man will flee from his brother

  35. 35

    وَأُمِّهِۦ وَأَبِيهِ

    And his mother and his father

  36. 36

    وَصَٰحِبَتِهِۦ وَبَنِيهِ

    And his wife and his children

  37. 37

    لِكُلِّ ٱمۡرِيࣲٕ مِّنۡهُمۡ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ شَأۡنࣱ يُغۡنِيهِ

    For every man, that Day, will be a matter adequate for him

  38. 38

    وُجُوهࣱ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ مُّسۡفِرَةࣱ‏

    [Some] faces, that Day, will be bright

  39. 39

    ضَاحِكَةࣱ مُّسۡتَبۡشِرَةࣱ‏

    Laughing, rejoicing at good news

  40. 40

    وَوُجُوهࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٍ عَلَيۡهَا غَبَرَةࣱ‏

    And [other] faces, that Day, will have upon them dust

  41. 41

    تَرۡهَقُهَا قَتَرَةٌ

    Blackness will cover them

  42. 42

    أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡكَفَرَةُ ٱلۡفَجَرَةُ

    Those are the disbelievers, the wicked ones
