Surah Al-Layl — The Night

سُوْرَةُ الَّيْلِ

Surah 92 of 11421 versesMeccan
Listen & read in the appSearch the Qur'an
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful
  1. 1

    وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا يَغۡشَىٰ

    By the night when it covers

  2. 2

    وَٱلنَّهَارِ إِذَا تَجَلَّىٰ

    And [by] the day when it appears

  3. 3

    وَمَا خَلَقَ ٱلذَّكَرَ وَٱلۡأُنثَىٰٓ

    And [by] He who created the male and female

  4. 4

    إِنَّ سَعۡيَكُمۡ لَشَتَّىٰ

    Indeed, your efforts are diverse

  5. 5

    فَأَمَّا مَنۡ أَعۡطَىٰ وَٱتَّقَىٰ

    As for he who gives and fears Allah

  6. 6

    وَصَدَّقَ بِٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ

    And believes in the best [reward]

  7. 7

    فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُۥ لِلۡيُسۡرَىٰ

    We will ease him toward ease

  8. 8

    وَأَمَّا مَنۢ بَخِلَ وَٱسۡتَغۡنَىٰ

    But as for he who withholds and considers himself free of need

  9. 9

    وَكَذَّبَ بِٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ

    And denies the best [reward]

  10. 10

    فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُۥ لِلۡعُسۡرَىٰ

    We will ease him toward difficulty

  11. 11

    وَمَا يُغۡنِي عَنۡهُ مَالُهُۥٓ إِذَا تَرَدَّىٰٓ

    And what will his wealth avail him when he falls

  12. 12

    إِنَّ عَلَيۡنَا لَلۡهُدَىٰ

    Indeed, [incumbent] upon Us is guidance

  13. 13

    وَإِنَّ لَنَا لَلۡأٓخِرَةَ وَٱلۡأُولَىٰ

    And indeed, to Us belongs the Hereafter and the first [life]

  14. 14

    فَأَنذَرۡتُكُمۡ نَارࣰ ا تَلَظَّىٰ

    So I have warned you of a Fire which is blazing

  15. 15

    لَا يَصۡلَىٰهَآ إِلَّا ٱلۡأَشۡقَى

    None will [enter to] burn therein except the most wretched one

  16. 16

    ٱلَّذِي كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ

    Who had denied and turned away

  17. 17

    وَسَيُجَنَّبُهَا ٱلۡأَتۡقَى

    But the righteous one will avoid it

  18. 18

    ٱلَّذِي يُؤۡتِي مَالَهُۥ يَتَزَكَّىٰ

    [He] who gives [from] his wealth to purify himself

  19. 19

    وَمَا لِأَحَدٍ عِندَهُۥ مِن نِّعۡمَةࣲ تُجۡزَىٰٓ

    And not [giving] for anyone who has [done him] a favor to be rewarded

  20. 20

    إِلَّا ٱبۡتِغَآءَ وَجۡهِ رَبِّهِ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰ

    But only seeking the countenance of his Lord, Most High

  21. 21

    وَلَسَوۡفَ يَرۡضَىٰ

    And he is going to be satisfied
