Surah Al-Infitar — The Cleaving

سُوْرَةُ الْاِنْفِطَارِ

Surah 82 of 11419 versesMeccan
Listen & read in the appSearch the Qur'an
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful
  1. 1

    إِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ ٱنفَطَرَتۡ

    When the sky breaks apart

  2. 2

    وَإِذَا ٱلۡكَوَاكِبُ ٱنتَثَرَتۡ

    And when the stars fall, scattering

  3. 3

    وَإِذَا ٱلۡبِحَارُ فُجِّرَتۡ

    And when the seas are erupted

  4. 4

    وَإِذَا ٱلۡقُبُورُ بُعۡثِرَتۡ

    And when the [contents of] graves are scattered

  5. 5

    عَلِمَتۡ نَفۡسࣱ مَّا قَدَّمَتۡ وَأَخَّرَتۡ

    A soul will [then] know what it has put forth and kept back

  6. 6

    يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَا غَرَّكَ بِرَبِّكَ ٱلۡكَرِيمِ

    O mankind, what has deceived you concerning your Lord, the Generous

  7. 7

    ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكَ فَسَوَّىٰكَ فَعَدَلَكَ

    Who created you, proportioned you, and balanced you

  8. 8

    فِيٓ أَيِّ صُورَةࣲ مَّا شَآءَ رَكَّبَكَ

    In whatever form He willed has He assembled you

  9. 9

    كَلَّا بَلۡ تُكَذِّبُونَ بِٱلدِّينِ

    No! But you deny the Recompense

  10. 10

    وَإِنَّ عَلَيۡكُمۡ لَحَٰفِظِينَ

    And indeed, [appointed] over you are keepers

  11. 11

    كِرَامࣰ ا كَٰتِبِينَ

    Noble and recording

  12. 12

    يَعۡلَمُونَ مَا تَفۡعَلُونَ

    They know whatever you do

  13. 13

    إِنَّ ٱلۡأَبۡرَارَ لَفِي نَعِيمࣲ‏

    Indeed, the righteous will be in pleasure

  14. 14

    وَإِنَّ ٱلۡفُجَّارَ لَفِي جَحِيمࣲ‏

    And indeed, the wicked will be in Hellfire

  15. 15

    يَصۡلَوۡنَهَا يَوۡمَ ٱلدِّينِ

    They will [enter to] burn therein on the Day of Recompense

  16. 16

    وَمَا هُمۡ عَنۡهَا بِغَآئِبِينَ

    And never therefrom will they be absent

  17. 17

    وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا يَوۡمُ ٱلدِّينِ

    And what can make you know what is the Day of Recompense

  18. 18

    ثُمَّ مَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا يَوۡمُ ٱلدِّينِ

    Then, what can make you know what is the Day of Recompense

  19. 19

    يَوۡمَ لَا تَمۡلِكُ نَفۡسࣱ لِّنَفۡسࣲ شَيۡـࣰٔ اۖ وَٱلۡأَمۡرُ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ لِّلَّهِ

    It is the Day when a soul will not possess for another soul [power to do] a thing; and the command, that Day, is [entirely] with Allah
