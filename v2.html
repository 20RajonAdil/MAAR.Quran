Install on iPhone / iPad

iOS doesn't use APK files — instead, Safari can add this site to your Home Screen as its own app icon, opening full-screen with no browser bar. This must be done in Safari (not Chrome or another browser) for it to work.

  1. 1
    Open this site in Safari (you're most of the way there already if you're reading this on your iPhone).
  2. 2
    Tap the Share icon in Safari's toolbar.
  3. 3
    Scroll down the share menu and tap "Add to Home Screen."
  4. 4
    Tap "Add" in the top right corner.
  5. 5
    A "Quran Maar" icon now appears on your Home Screen and opens like a normal app.

Install on Desktop / Laptop / Mac

No download needed on desktop either — Quran Maar is a PWA (Progressive Web App), so your browser can install it directly from this page as a real, standalone app with its own window and icon.

  1. 1
    Look for an install icon (often a small monitor with a down arrow, or a "+") at the right side of your browser's address bar — in Chrome, Edge, or Brave this appears automatically on supported sites.
  2. 2
    Click it, then click "Install." If you don't see the icon, open the browser's ⋮ menu and look for "Install Quran Maar…" or "Add to Home screen."
  3. 3
    On a Mac using Safari: open the File menu and choose "Add to Dock."
  4. 4
    Quran Maar now opens in its own window from your desktop, taskbar, dock, or Start menu, without any browser tabs or address bar.
